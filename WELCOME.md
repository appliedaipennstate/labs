# Welcome to Applied AI Labs

You're inside a **Codespace**: a coding workspace that runs in your browser. Nothing gets installed on your laptop.

## Tonight: put your card on the Labs wall

1. Open the Copilot chat. If it isn't open already, click the chat icon at the top of the window.
2. Set the chat to **Agent**.
3. Send this: **Make my Labs card**

Copilot will ask you three questions, write your card, and send it to the club. Each time it wants to run a command, it tells you what the command does. Read it, then allow it.

Watch your card show up: https://labs.appliedaipennstate.com/wall/

## Four ideas you'll use tonight

- **Repository:** a project's folder, plus the history of every change ever made to it. This one belongs to the club.
- **Codespace:** the workspace you're in right now. A computer in the cloud with the project already on it.
- **Agent:** an AI that does the work instead of just answering. It edits files and runs commands, and it asks you before each step.
- **Pull request:** how you ask a project you don't own to accept your change. The change gets reviewed, then merged in.

## If Copilot isn't working

Click in the terminal at the bottom of the window and type:

    labs new

Open the card it makes in the `members` folder, replace the three parts in [square brackets], and save. Then type:

    labs submit

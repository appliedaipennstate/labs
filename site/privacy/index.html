Privacy Policy

Applied AI Labs platform. Effective 2026-10-16.

Who runs this

The Applied AI Labs platform is run by the Applied AI Club at Penn State, a student-run organization. It is not operated or endorsed by Penn State University, Microsoft or Flare. Questions about this policy: hello@appliedaipennstate.com.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture link, which Google shares when you choose to sign in. We ask for nothing else from your Google account.
  • Inside the platform: your role and which workspaces you belong to, and the work done there: workflows, spreadsheets imported into a workspace, calculated results, reports and run history.
  • Demonstration data only for now. This release runs on made-up members and numbers. Real student records are not loaded until the club, its advisor and the university's review path have approved it.

How we use it

To sign you in, to decide who can see which workspace, and to run and show the results you ask for. We do not sell information, we do not run advertising, and we do not use member records to train AI models.

Where it is stored and who handles it

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and server functions (Amazon Web Services, US East).
  • Cloudflare serves the website.
  • Google provides the sign-in.

The site has no analytics or advertising trackers. Your browser keeps a sign-in session in its local storage so you stay signed in. Each workspace is separated, and the database enforces that people see only the workspaces they belong to.

AI features

AI assistance is switched off. If the club turns it on later, administrators will approve it first, the page will say what data a step sends, and it will send only what that step needs.

Google user data

The platform's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers listed above, as needed to run the platform, or when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting

We keep account and workspace records while your access is active. To ask what we hold about you, or to have your account and its records deleted, email hello@appliedaipennstate.com. We aim to answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

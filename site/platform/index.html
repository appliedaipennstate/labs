Applied AI Labs platform

A web app where officers of student organizations build, test and review the automations behind their routine work, such as a weekly membership points and status report.

Sign in to the Labs platform

Access is by invitation. Sign-in uses your Google account.

What you can do

  • Import a spreadsheet, check it for problems and see exactly how each result was worked out.
  • Test a workflow safely before anything is applied, then review and approve changes.
  • Download clean reports and look back at every run.

Who runs it

The platform is built and run by the Applied AI Club at Penn State, a student-run organization. It is not operated or endorsed by Penn State University, Microsoft or Flare. This release uses made-up demonstration data.
